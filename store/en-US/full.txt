Your car's FM tuner doesn't pick up the stations you want? Play them from your phone over Bluetooth, with the station name and song on the car display.

IN THE CAR
• Auto-start: the radio turns on by itself when your car connects over Bluetooth and pauses when you turn the engine off.
• Steering wheel buttons switch stations; the car display shows the song, artist and station.
• Driving mode: huge buttons, the screen stays on, swipe for the next station.
• Calls and navigation: the radio pauses for a call and gets quieter while the navigator speaks.

WHEN THE SIGNAL IS BAD
• Buffer of 10–60 seconds, or up to 3 minutes in Highway mode — the music keeps playing through tunnels and dead zones.
• Quiet automatic reconnecting, and a countdown of how long the reserve will last.
• Backup links: if a stream dies, it switches to another link of the same station; on a weak connection it picks a lighter one.
• “Go live” — reset the buffer and catch up with the broadcast without a gap.

SOUND
• Smooth crossfade between stations — no silence while the next one loads.
• Volume leveling between stations and an equalizer with car presets.

STATIONS
• Search the open Radio Browser catalog by name, with popular stations from your country.
• Add any stream by link (.mp3, .aac, .m3u, .pls, .m3u8) — it's checked before saving.
• Station logos are found automatically; four list layouts.

AND ALSO

• Listening stats and a data usage counter.
